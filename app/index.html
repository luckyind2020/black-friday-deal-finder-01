Real deals, checked against the stores — with an honest verdict on every one. A small discount on something you wanted anyway is not a doorbuster, and this finder says so.

How these deals were checked

Every price on this page was reported by a named public source — a retailer announcement, a deals tracker (Tom's Guide / TechRadar, 9to5Toys), or a Black Friday ad scan — and checked on 27 November 2026. Each deal card names its source and its store, and links to the store's own search page for that product, so you can confirm the price before you buy. Prices move fast on Black Friday weekend and stock runs out; the store's page is the final word.

The verdict is computed from the numbers: 50%+ off is a real deal, 30–49% a good deal, 15–29% a fair deal, under 15% a weak deal. A “lowest tracked” tag means the source called it the lowest price it had seen for that product. No deal here was invented, and no price is from an earlier sale.
